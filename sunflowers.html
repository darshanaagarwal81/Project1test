Sunflowers

Sunflowers are cheerful, tall, and iconic. Their bright yellow petals and dark centers mimic the sun, making them a symbol of happiness, loyalty, and warmth.

How They Follow the Sun

Young sunflowers exhibit heliotropism, turning toward the sun throughout the day. Once they mature, they generally face east to attract pollinators in the morning.

Fun Facts

  • Sunflowers can grow over 10 feet tall (and some varieties even taller).
  • Each sunflower head contains hundreds to thousands of seeds arranged in a spiral pattern based on the golden ratio.
  • Sunflower seeds are used for food, oil, and bird feed.

Growing Tips

Plant sunflowers in full sun with well-draining soil. Space seeds 12–18 inches apart, and support tall varieties with stakes.
