Orchids

Orchids are among the most diverse and exotic flowers on Earth. Their intricate shapes and long-lasting blooms make them prized in both gardening and floral design.

Orchid Diversity

There are over 25,000 orchid species, ranging from tiny blooms to large, dramatic flowers. They are found on every continent except Antarctica.

Fun Facts

  • Some orchids mimic insects to attract pollinators.
  • The vanilla bean comes from an orchid (Vanilla planifolia).
  • Orchids can live for many years; some bloom annually while others bloom only once every few years.

Care Tips

Orchids prefer bright, indirect light. Use a well-draining potting mix, water when the medium is nearly dry, and provide good air circulation.
