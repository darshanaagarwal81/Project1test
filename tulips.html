Tulips

Tulips are a beloved symbol of spring. Their clean, cup-shaped blooms and rich palette make them favorites for gardens and bouquets.

A Brief History

Tulips originated in Central Asia and were brought to Europe in the 16th century. The Dutch famously created "tulip mania," one of history's first speculative bubbles.

Fun Facts

  • There are over 100 species of wild tulips and thousands of cultivated varieties.
  • Tulips can be grown from bulbs and often return year after year in the same spot.
  • In the language of flowers, different tulip colors can mean love, forgiveness, and happiness.

Planting Tips

Plant tulip bulbs in fall, 6–8 inches deep, in well-draining soil. Provide full sun and allow foliage to die back naturally after bloom.
