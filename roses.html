Roses

Roses are one of the most popular and celebrated flowers worldwide. Their fragrance and color variety make them a timeless symbol of love, passion, and appreciation.

History & Symbolism

Roses have been cultivated for thousands of years, with roots in ancient Persia, China, and Rome. Each color holds its own meaning: red for love, yellow for friendship, white for purity, and pink for gratitude.

Fun Facts

  • There are over 300 species of roses and thousands of cultivars.
  • Roses are edible; rose petals are used in teas, syrups, and desserts.
  • The oldest rose fossil is 35 million years old.

Care Tips

To keep roses blooming: plant in full sun, give them well-draining soil, prune regularly, and water deeply but infrequently.
